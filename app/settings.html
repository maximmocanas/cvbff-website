Welcome to CvBFF — let's get you set up

Fill in your details once and CvBFF uses them to generate a tailored one-page CV for every job you apply for. It takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Work experience — add every role and write a detailed description of what you did, the tools you used, and your wins. The more detail, the better the output.
  2. Skills bank — list every real skill and tool you have. Be broad: hard skills (Excel, SQL) and soft skills (stakeholder management).
  3. Personal details — name, email, and location for the CV header.
  4. Click Save details below when you're done.
