FAQ

Common questions

What does CvBFF actually change in my CV?
+
CvBFF rewrites your bullet points to mirror the language and priorities in the job posting, reorders sections by relevance, and adjusts how much space each role gets based on how relevant it is. Your facts stay the same, it's the framing that changes.
Does it make things up?
+
No. CvBFF only works with what you've added to your profile. It reframes and reprioritises, it doesn't invent experience or skills you don't have. You're in control of what goes into your profile.
What is a credit and when is it spent?
+
One credit unlocks one document, either a full CV or a cover letter. You can generate and preview content for free. You only spend a credit when you click "Unlock" to reveal the full document and enable the PDF download.
Is my data safe?
+
Your profile and job posting text are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the CV. Anthropic does not use API data for training. Your profile is stored securely in Supabase with Row Level Security. We don't sell data or run ads. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
Does it work on any job site?
+
Yes, CvBFF reads the text of whatever page you're on. It works on LinkedIn, Indeed, company careers pages, and anywhere else a job posting lives in plain text. You can also paste the job description directly into the extension if the page is behind a login.
Do credits expire?
+
No. Credits stay on your account until you use them. Buy when you need them and use them at your own pace.
Will my employer know I used AI to write my CV?
+
No. The output is a clean, professional document, your name, your experience, your words, just better framed. There's nothing in the file that indicates AI was involved. Tailoring your CV to each job is exactly what careers advisors have always recommended; CvBFF just makes it fast.
Why not just use ChatGPT?
+
You can, but you'll spend 20 minutes crafting the right prompt, reformatting the output, pasting it back, and fixing the layout every single time. CvBFF is built specifically for this workflow: your profile is already saved, it reads the job posting automatically, it knows how to budget space per role, and it downloads as a properly formatted PDF in one click.
